# NovaMart Customer Support - Knowledge Base

NovaMart is an online store for electronics, gadgets and accessories that delivers across India.
This FAQ is the only source the support chatbot is allowed to answer from.
Each "Keywords" line lists other words customers use for the same question; it helps the search but is not part of the answer.

## Orders

### Q1. How do I track my order?
Go to **My Account > My Orders** and select the order to see its live status. When your order ships, we also send an SMS and email with a tracking link. Tracking details can take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch. Every order has an order ID made of "NM" followed by 8 digits, for example NM10293847.
Keywords: track, tracking, where is my order, order status, shipped, order ID

### Q2. Can I cancel my order?
You can cancel an order free of charge from **My Orders > Cancel** until it ships. Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled; you can refuse the delivery or return the item after it arrives instead. For prepaid orders, the refund for a cancelled order is started within 24 hours.
Keywords: cancel, cancellation, stop my order, don't want it anymore

### Q3. Can I change the delivery address after placing an order?
The delivery address can be changed only before the order ships. Go to **My Orders > Change address**, or contact support if the option is not shown. After dispatch the address cannot be changed, but you can reschedule the delivery date using the link in your tracking SMS.
Keywords: change address, wrong address, update address, edit order, reschedule

## Shipping and Delivery

### Q4. What are the delivery options and charges?
- Standard delivery: 3 to 5 business days. Free on orders of ₹499 or more, otherwise ₹40.
- Express delivery: 1 to 2 business days for ₹99, available in most cities.
- Same-day delivery: ₹149 in Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Pune for orders placed before 12 noon.

The delivery date shown at checkout is an estimate and does not count Sundays or public holidays.
Keywords: shipping cost, delivery charge, delivery fee, how long does delivery take, fast delivery, free shipping, express, same day

### Q5. Do you deliver to my area? Do you ship internationally?
We deliver to more than 19,000 PIN codes across India. Enter your PIN code on any product page to check if delivery and Cash on Delivery are available there. We do not ship outside India at the moment.
Keywords: pincode, pin code, location, city, international shipping, abroad, outside India, serviceable

### Q6. My order is late, or it says delivered but I did not receive it. What should I do?
If your order has not arrived 2 days after the expected delivery date, contact support with your order ID and we will follow up with the courier. If tracking shows "Delivered" but you did not get the package, first check with neighbours or your building security, then report it within 48 hours from **My Orders > Report an issue**. The investigation takes 3 to 5 business days, after which we send a replacement or a full refund.
Keywords: delayed, late delivery, not received, not arrived, missing package, lost parcel, marked delivered

## Returns and Refunds

### Q7. What is your return policy?
Most items can be returned within 10 days of delivery if they are unused and sent back in the original packaging with all accessories, tags and the invoice. Mobile phones, laptops and tablets have a 7-day replacement-only policy that covers manufacturing defects. These items cannot be returned: in-ear earphones once the seal is opened, software, gift cards, and products damaged by misuse.
Keywords: return window, return period, returnable, non-returnable, how many days to return, return rules

### Q8. How do I return an item?
1. Go to **My Orders**, choose the item and click **Return**.
2. Pick a reason and a pickup slot. Return pickup is free.
3. Pack the item in its original box with all accessories and the invoice.

Our courier collects the item within 2 business days, and you get an email when the pickup is done.
Keywords: return process, start a return, return a product, send back, return pickup, return request, how to return

### Q9. When will I get my refund?
Refunds start once the returned item passes a quality check, usually within 2 business days of pickup. After that the money reaches you in:
- UPI and NovaMart Wallet: 1 to 3 business days
- Credit cards, debit cards and net banking: 5 to 7 business days
- Cash on Delivery orders: instantly to your NovaMart Wallet, or 5 to 7 business days to a bank account you add

Keywords: refund status, refund time, money back, how long for refund, COD refund, reimbursement

### Q10. I received a damaged, defective or wrong item. What do I do?
Report it within 48 hours of delivery from **My Orders > Report an issue** and attach photos of the product and the package. Once we verify it, you can choose a free replacement or a full refund. You do not need to wait for a return pickup to raise the request.
Keywords: broken, damaged, cracked, scratched, defective, faulty, wrong product, wrong item, not working, stopped working

### Q11. Can I exchange a product for a different size or colour?
Exchanges are available for the same product in a different size or colour within 10 days of delivery, as long as it is in stock. To switch to a different model, return the item for a refund and place a new order.
Keywords: exchange, swap, replace with another, different colour, different size, different model

## Payments and Offers

### Q12. Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard and RuPay), net banking, NovaMart Wallet and Cash on Delivery. Cash on Delivery is available for orders up to ₹10,000 and has a ₹20 handling fee.
Keywords: pay, payment options, payment modes, COD, cash on delivery, UPI, credit card, debit card

### Q13. Money was deducted but my order was not placed. What happens now?
If a payment fails after the money is deducted, the amount is refunded automatically to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. If it has not arrived by then, contact support with the transaction ID (UTR number) from your bank statement.
Keywords: payment failed, amount debited, money deducted, charged twice, double payment, transaction failed

### Q14. Do you offer EMI?
Yes. No-cost EMI for 3 or 6 months is available on orders above ₹3,000 with credit cards from our partner banks. Standard EMI for up to 12 months is available on most credit cards at the bank's interest rate. Choose **EMI** at checkout to see the plans for your card.
Keywords: EMI, instalments, installments, monthly payments, no cost EMI, pay later

### Q15. How do I use a coupon code?
Enter the code in the **Apply coupon** box on the checkout page. Only one coupon can be used per order, and coupons cannot be used to buy gift cards. If a coupon does not work, check its expiry date and minimum order value in **My Account > Coupons**.
Keywords: promo code, discount code, voucher, offer, coupon not working

## Account, Warranty and Contact

### Q16. I forgot my password or cannot log in. What should I do?
Click **Forgot password** on the login page and we will email you a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. You can also log in with a one-time password (OTP) sent to your registered mobile number. After 5 wrong password attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes for security.
Keywords: login problem, sign in, reset password, locked account, OTP

### Q17. Do products come with a warranty?
All products come with the manufacturer's warranty, usually 1 year, as shown on the product page. Warranty claims are handled by the brand's authorised service centres, so keep your NovaMart invoice as proof of purchase. You can add NovaMart Care extended warranty for 1 or 2 extra years within 30 days of delivery.
Keywords: guarantee, repair, get it repaired, service centre, extended warranty, NovaMart Care

- Email: support@novamart.example (we reply within 24 hours)
- Phone: 1800-123-4567 (toll-free), 9 AM to 9 PM every day

Keep your order ID ready so we can help you faster.
